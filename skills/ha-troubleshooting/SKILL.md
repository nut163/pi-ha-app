# Troubleshooting loop

Reproduce the symptom, inspect current state, then read focused recent logs.
Separate evidence from hypotheses. Prefer a reversible diagnostic or targeted
configuration change. After a mutation, re-check health and state; do not hide
an unresolved error behind a restart.
